 1. PROJECT OVERVIEW
We are looking for an experienced WordPress designer and developer to create a modern, professional,
responsive and user-friendly website for 2AEU KGO, the alumni association of Université Peleforo Gon Coulibaly
(formerly URES Korhogo), Côte d'Ivoire.
The association was legally constituted on 29 August 2026 and is governed by an elected Executive Board. Our
members live in Korhogo, in Abidjan, across Côte d'Ivoire and abroad. The website will become the official home
of the association.
Website language: FRENCH. All content is supplied by us in French, and the entire interface menus,
forms, error messages, automated e-mails must be correctly labelled in French. The technical structure
must allow an English version to be added later. You may communicate with us in English or French.

Purpose of the website
   •    Strengthen the visibility and credibility of the association.
   •    Connect former students of URES Korhogo across promotions and countries.
   •    Present the association's mission, activities and achievements.
   •    Promote professional, educational and networking opportunities.
   •    Encourage new members to join and facilitate the payment of membership fees online.
   •    Attract institutional, professional and financial partners.
   •    Give members access to useful information and official documents.

 6. MEMBERSHIP AND PAYMENT SYSTEM
Membership application form
   •      Fields: surname, first names, date and place of birth, nationality, ID document type and number, address,
          city, country, telephone, WhatsApp, e-mail, faculty or programme, degree, graduation year, promotion,
          occupation, employer.
   •      Upload of a passport photograph and of an identity document.
   •      Beneficiaries section (spouse, children) as provided by our Internal Rules.
   •      Mandatory checkbox: acceptance of the Statutes and the Internal Rules.
   •      Automatic confirmation e-mail with receipt; notification to the Treasurer and the Secretary General.

Payment
   •      Payment gateway: CinetPay or PayDunya. State which you recommend and why.
   •      Must accept mobile money: Wave, Orange Money, MTN MoMo, Moov Money and Visa/Mastercard, since
          some of our members live abroad.
   •      Payments recorded automatically against the member's account; receipts generated and e-mailed.
   •      Administrators must be able to record an offline payment (cash or bank transfer) manually.
   •      Test transactions must be demonstrated in a staging environment before go-live.
   •      The cost of integrating the payment solution must be stated SEPARATELY in your proposal, including any
          transaction fees and who bears them.

 8. SECURITY REQUIREMENTS (MANDATORY)
The site will hold personal data of members including identity documents and will process payments. Security is
not optional. You must implement and document all of the following:
   •   Web application firewall and malware scanning (Wordfence, Sucuri or equivalent), with alerting.
   •   SSL/HTTPS enforced across the whole site, with automatic certificate renewal.
   •   Two-factor authentication (2FA) for all administrator and editor accounts.
   •   Custom login URL, limited login attempts and automatic lockout after repeated failures.
   •   Strong password policy for all privileged accounts.
   •   Automated daily backups stored off-site (UpdraftPlus or equivalent), with a documented restore procedure
       and one restore test performed in front of us.
   •   Activity log recording every administrative action (WP Activity Log or equivalent).
   •   Anti-spam and CAPTCHA on all public forms (Akismet, CleanTalk, hCaptcha or Turnstile).
   •   Protection of uploaded files: no public directory listing, restricted access to identity documents and
       photographs.
   •   Database hardening: changed table prefix, file editing disabled in the dashboard, hardened wp-config.
   •   Security headers (HSTS, X-Frame-Options, Content-Security-Policy); XML-RPC disabled if not required.
   •   Strict role-based access control, applied as set out in section 5.
   •   Cookie consent system and GDPR-compatible management of personal data, including a documented
       procedure to delete a member's data on request.
   •   All plugins and themes from reputable sources, licensed and kept updatable.
